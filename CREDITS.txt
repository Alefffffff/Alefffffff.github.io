Shore animation experiment 02

Adapted from "Sea Waves on the Beach Shore" by Eduardo Allegrini:
https://codepen.io/edalgrin/pen/jOMQJBK

Uses the reference's quadratic wave segments, layered foam/water passes and
reversing tide concept. Adapted to plain Canvas without React, bounded to the
portfolio divider, with time-based motion, reduced-motion support, pause while
the page is hidden, and horizontal separators on mobile.

Portfolio artwork and media: Yuyang (Alef) Liu.
https://www.artstation.com/alefliu
